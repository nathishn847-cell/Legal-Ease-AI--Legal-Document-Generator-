Project Specification & Assessment Viva Dossier

Topic: LegalEase - AI-Powered Legal Document Generator | Track: Google Cloud Generative AI

1. Problem Statement

In contemporary legal and business ecosystems, individuals, tenants, freelancers, and small startups frequently face severe hurdles in obtaining legally enforceable agreements. Hiring advocate counsel to draft or vet standard agreements costs between ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 per contract, leading over 85% of people to either download faulty online templates or sign predatory, biased agreements containing unfair liabilities, non-competes, and forfeiture traps.

2. Proposed Solution & Project Objectives

  • Zero-Shot AI Legal Document Generation: Autonomously generate customized, mutually equitable legal agreements (NDAs, Leases, Freelance, Employment) within 5 seconds based on user parameters.
  • Automated Risk Exposure Scoring (0-100): Implement an algorithmic composite risk metric factoring in unilateral indemnity, notice asymmetry, and penalty caps.
  • Plain-English Translation: Deconstruct complex legalese into layman's terms for ordinary citizens.
  • Interactive RAG Legal Copilot: Query contracts in conversational English powered by Google Gemini 1.5.
  • Cloud Scalability: Serverless deployment on Google Cloud Run utilizing Vertex AI and Document AI OCR.

3. Viva Voce / Assessment Panel Questions & Answers

Q1: How does your AI ensure generated contracts are legally sound and not one-sided?

Ans: LegalEase implements a Dual-Engine Architecture: First, the Generation module injects prompt constraints based on the Indian Contract Act 1872 (Section 10 & Section 27) guaranteeing reciprocity. Second, the generated draft is automatically passed to our Semantic Risk Auditor to verify that the Risk Score is below 20 (Safe Rating) before finalization.

Q2: Why choose Google Cloud Vertex AI / Gemini over generic OpenAI models?

Ans: Vertex AI Gemini 1.5 offers an unprecedented 1-Million token context window, allowing entire 100-page loan agreements to be ingested in one prompt without loss of context. Furthermore, GCP guarantees enterprise privacy: customer data is never used to train foundational models.

Q3: How does LegalEase handle scanned or handwritten legal stamp papers?

Ans: LegalEase uses Google Cloud Document AI (Contract Processor & Form Parser), which leverages deep learning vision transformers to recognize tables, notary stamps, and paragraph hierarchies before sending text to Gemini.

Q4: Does LegalEase replace a licensed advocate?

Ans: No. LegalEase operates as an Automated Legal Co-Pilot / First-Pass Drafting & Audit System. It democratizes contract drafting for everyday citizens and prepares users before formal legal registration.
